How to join

Modded servers need the matching CurseForge pack and a Discord whitelist. Vanilla is just the address. Valheim is the join code. Palworld is in the community server list. Counter-Strike 2 uses a console connect command. 7 Days to Die is in the in-game server browser. Rust is in the Community tab. Terraria joins by IP. Project Zomboid is in the in-game or Steam server browser. Garry's Mod uses a console connect command or Steam Favorites. DayZ is in the in-game or Steam server browser.

Modded servers

All the Mods 10 and Create: Above and Beyond.

  1. Install CurseForge and the matching modpack.
  2. Launch that pack, add the server address from the card, and connect.
  3. Join Discord and open #link-account. Verify and link your Minecraft account there to get whitelisted.
  • All the Mods 10 on CurseForge (opens in a new tab)
  • Create: Above and Beyond on CurseForge (opens in a new tab)

Vanilla Survival

Paper, castle spawn, open server.

  1. Open Minecraft Java Edition.
  2. Choose Multiplayer, add a server, and paste the Vanilla address.
  3. Join. There is no whitelist.

Valheim

Crossplay.

  1. Open Valheim. Steam and Xbox can join the same world.
  2. Choose Join Game and enter the code on the Valheim card.

Palworld

PvE, crossplay, no password, up to 20 players.

  1. Open Palworld and choose Join Multiplayer Game.
  2. Open Community Servers and search for Flint and Steel.
  3. Or paste the address from the Palworld card into the connect box at the bottom.

Counter-Strike 2

Casual and deathmatch, up to 20 players.

  1. Install Counter-Strike 2 on Steam.
  2. Enable the developer console in Settings > Game.
  3. Press ~ to open the console.
  4. Paste the connect command from the Counter-Strike 2 card.

7 Days to Die

Vanilla V 3.4, PvE co-op, up to 16 players. No password.

  1. Open 7 Days to Die.
  2. Search the in-game server browser for Flint and Steel. The listing is Flint and Steel Network | 7 Days to Die PvE.
  3. Or choose Connect to IP and paste the address from the card. The port is 28246.

Rust

Vanilla PvP, 50 slots, no password.

  1. Open Rust and search for Flint and Steel in the Community tab of the server browser.
  2. Or press F1 and paste the connect command from the Rust card.
  3. Use #rust-info, #rust-chat, and #rust-support on the Flint and Steel Discord.

Terraria

1.4.5.8 on TShock, Classic, 16 slots, no password.

  1. Open Terraria and choose Multiplayer, then Join via IP.
  2. Enter terraria.flintandsteelnetwork.com and port 4379. There is no password.
  3. The world is the large Classic world FlintAndSteel.

Project Zomboid

Build 42 vanilla PvE, 32 slots, no password.

  1. Open Project Zomboid and choose Direct Connect, or add the address to Favorites.
  2. Use zomboid.flintandsteelnetwork.com:57257. There is no password.
  3. Or find Flint and Steel Network | Project Zomboid in the in-game or Steam server browser.

Garry's Mod

Sandbox on gm_construct, 24 slots, no password.

  1. Open Garry's Mod.
  2. Press ~ to open the console and paste the connect command from the Garry's Mod card.
  3. Or add gmod.flintandsteelnetwork.com:61937 to Steam Favorites.

DayZ

Vanilla PvP on Chernarus, 40 slots, no password.

  1. Open DayZ and choose Direct Connect, or add the address to Favorites.
  2. Use dayz.flintandsteelnetwork.com:32783. There is no password.
  3. Or search Flint and Steel Network | DayZ Vanilla in the in-game or Steam server browser.

Reputation

Ranks

Member comes with Discord verify. The rest is time, a staff pick, or a staff role.

01

Member

Granted on Discord verify.

02

Veteran

Automatic after 50 hours played: 400 claimed chunks and /fly.

03

VIP

Hand-picked by staff.

04

Moderator

Staff who help keep chat and the worlds fair.

05

Admin

Runs the network.

House rules

Rules

Play like someone else is building next to you.

  1. Be respectful. Harassment, slurs, and personal attacks are not welcome.
  2. No griefing and no stealing.
  3. No cheats, hacked clients, or x-ray.
  4. No lag machines or anything built to drag the server down.
  5. Keep chat friendly.
  6. Listen to staff.
